## 2. What we collect

We collect only the data we need to make the app work.

### 2.1 Account data
- Email address
- Chosen username
- An account identifier (UUID) generated by our servers
- A hashed representation of your password (we never store or transmit
  the plaintext password)

### 2.2 Content you create
- Meal titles, ingredients, instructions, and any notes you type
- Photos you upload for a meal (stored on Cloudinary)
- The weekly meal plan you build, including which meals are on which
  days
- Pantry items and shopping list entries

### 2.3 Optional device data
- **Camera imagery** — only while you actively use the barcode scanner
  or QR-code scanner. The camera stream is decoded on-device and is
  never recorded, stored, or transmitted.
- **Bluetooth information** — only while you actively use “Share via
  Bluetooth”. Nearby-device identifiers stay on your device; we do
  not use Bluetooth to derive location.

### 2.4 Data we do NOT collect
- Precise or approximate location
- Your contacts, calendar, or files outside what you explicitly upload
- Advertising identifiers
- SMS or call metadata
- Health, fitness, or biometric data
- Financial or payment information (Forkcast is free)

## 3. How we use the data

- **Provide the service** — signing you in, syncing your meals and
  plans across your devices, generating a shopping list from your
  planner.
- **Improve reliability** — diagnosing bugs when you report them (we
  will ask you for consent before requesting logs).
- **Send transactional email** — only when necessary: password reset
  emails, account deletion confirmations. We do NOT send marketing
  email.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data to train third-party
AI models. Meal ideas generated by our AI assistant are produced from
the pantry contents you’ve added and the meals you’ve saved; the
request is stateless and the assistant provider does not retain your
inputs beyond the immediate response.

## 5. How long we keep it

- Your account data is kept while your account is active.
- If you delete a meal, it’s removed from our database immediately;
  the associated Cloudinary asset is scheduled for deletion within 30
  days.
- If you delete your account (see §6), we remove all personal data
  within 30 days, except where we’re legally required to retain a
  minimal audit record.

Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA,
etc.) you have some or all of the following rights:

- **Access** — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
- **Correction** — ask us to fix inaccurate data.
- **Deletion** — ask us to delete your account and all associated
  data.
- **Portability** — request a machine-readable export.
- **Objection / restriction** — tell us to stop processing your data
  for a specific purpose.

To exercise any of these, email **TODO_CONTACT_EMAIL** from the
email address associated with your account. We’ll respond within 30
