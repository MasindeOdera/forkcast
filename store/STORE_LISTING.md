## Full description (4000 chars max)

```
Forkcast is the friendliest way to keep your kitchen organised.

Plan the week, capture the recipes you actually cook, share them with
friends, and let Forkcast help you shop for the ingredients you're
missing.

——————————————————————————————————————————

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• CREATE MEALS with photos, ingredients, and step-by-step
  instructions. Everything you write is yours; edit or delete
  whenever you want.

• DISCOVER what other Forkcast cooks are making. Copy any meal into
  your own collection with one tap, then tweak it to your taste.

• PLAN YOUR WEEK on a drag-and-drop calendar. Move meals between days
  until the shopping list is exactly what you want.

• KITCHEN TOOLS
    – Scan barcodes with your phone's camera to add pantry items
      instantly.
    – Watch expiry dates — Forkcast surfaces what's going off soon.
    – Build a shopping list from any meal or the whole week's plan.

• SHARE PLANS by QR code, native share sheet, or Bluetooth peer-to-peer
  — no account needed for the person receiving it.

• AI-ASSISTED IDEAS. Stuck for what to cook? Forkcast can suggest
  meals based on what's already in your pantry and what you've cooked
  recently.

———————————————————————————————————————————

WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT

• Clean, distraction-free interface.
• Works offline for browsing meals you've already saved.
• No adverts. No selling your data.
• Camera and Bluetooth are optional — the whole app works without them.
• Photos hosted on Cloudinary; meal data stored securely in the cloud.

———————————————————————————————————————————

PRIVACY

Forkcast collects only what it needs to sync your meals and photos
across devices. You can delete your account and everything associated
with it from Settings → Account → Delete.

Full policy: https://forkcast-six.vercel.app/privacy

———————————————————————————————————————————

CONTACT & FEEDBACK

Found a bug? Have a feature request?
Email: support@forkcast.app  (← change to your real address)

Happy cooking!
```

(Under 1800 chars, well within the 4000-char limit.)
